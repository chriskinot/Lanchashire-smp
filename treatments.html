Treatment 01

Full Head Transformation

Complete scalp coverage, rebuilt layer by layer for a natural, close-cropped finish.

From
£1,800
Sessions
3–4
Duration
2–4 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Full head transformation is the most comprehensive form of scalp micropigmentation. It covers the whole visible scalp — crown, mid-scalp, temples and hairline — and is built up gradually across several sessions so we can judge density and tone in real light as the skin settles.

The finish is designed to look like a shaved head of hair at around a No. 1 or No. 2 grade. Pigment is deposited in thousands of tiny impressions, each matched to the natural direction and diameter of a follicle, so from arm's length it dissolves into the scalp and from conversational distance it reads simply as closely cropped hair.

Every hairline is hand-mapped first — never drawn from a template. We work with the geometry of your face, your existing bone structure and your natural growth pattern, then ask you to approve the shape in a mirror before any pigment touches the skin.

What's included

  • Private 45-minute consultation
  • Hand-drawn hairline & approval
  • Three to four layering sessions
  • Free four-week top-up
  • Twelve-month refinement
  • Written aftercare plan

Best for

  • Significant hair loss across the top or crown
  • A shaved-head look with a more defined hairline
  • Alopecia areata or chemotherapy-related hair loss
  • Post-transplant density and full coverage
  • Anyone wanting a complete visual reset

Treatment 02

Hair Density Treatment

Pigment blended between existing follicles to restore the look of fuller, thicker hair.

From
£900
Sessions
2–3
Duration
2–3 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Hair density treatment is the opposite of a full head transformation: rather than cover a bald scalp, we work between the hair you already have. The result is that fine, sparser areas fill in visually, and the whole head looks a grade or two fuller.

Typical candidates are diffuse thinners, people with early-stage recession, or those who've had a transplant and want to add density between grafts. The finish is designed so you can still see individual hairs — but the scalp-to-hair ratio changes noticeably.

Because existing hair is precious, we're careful: pigment is placed in the scalp between follicles, never on top of them, and we work conservatively in the first session to judge colour and density in real daylight before adding more.

What's included

  • Consultation & scalp check
  • Two to three layered sessions
  • Free four-week top-up
  • Twelve-month refinement
  • Written aftercare plan
  • No shaving required

Best for

  • Diffuse thinning across the scalp
  • Early-stage recession where coverage is patchy
  • Adding density between transplanted grafts
  • Fine, light hair that lacks visual body
  • Softening the contrast between scalp and hairline

The hairline is the single strongest visual anchor on the face. When it recedes, the eye reads the whole head differently. Hairline reconstruction restores the shape of that anchor — not by filling a bald patch, but by rebuilding the transition between forehead and hair.

We work in soft, feathered layers at the front edge — never a hard line — and taper the density so the finish reads as your own natural growth pattern. A hairline should look like it belongs to your face, not like it was placed on top of it.

Before any pigment goes in, we map the new hairline with a cosmetic pencil and ask you to sit with it in a mirror, in daylight, from several angles. Only when you're happy does the first session begin.

What's included

  • Consultation & facial mapping
  • Hand-drawn hairline approval
  • Two to three sessions

Best for

  • A naturally high hairline you'd like lowered subtly
  • Blending the hairline after a transplant
  • A denser, more defined front edge
  • A hairline designed for your face — not copied from anyone else's

Treatment 04

Scar Camouflage

Softening the appearance of FUT, FUE and trauma scarring with colour-matched pigment.

From
£450
Sessions
1–3
Duration
1–2 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Scar tissue sits flat, lighter and shinier than the surrounding skin. Hair doesn't grow through it. The result is a bright, contrast-heavy patch the eye is drawn to instantly — and which you probably think about far more often than anyone else does.

Scar camouflage uses pigment matched to your surrounding skin and hair to break up that contrast. We don't try to erase the scar — that isn't realistic — but we soften its edges, reduce the shine, and overlay tiny follicle-like dots so that from a normal distance the eye reads hair rather than scar.

We work on FUT strips, FUE dotting, acne scars, burns and trauma scars. If the scar is raised or keloid, we may ask you to speak to a dermatologist first — pigment can still help, but the treatment plan changes.

What's included

  • Scar assessment & planning
  • Colour-matching to surrounding skin
  • One to three layered sessions
  • Four-week review appointment
  • Patch testing where needed
  • Written aftercare plan

Best for

  • A linear FUT strip scar from a transplant
  • FUE dotting you want blended with surrounding hair
  • Trauma, burn and surgical scars on the scalp
  • Acne scarring on the scalp or jawline
  • Facelift or brow-lift scars you want softened

Treatment 05

Beard & Facial Hair

Densifying patchy beards, sideburns and jawlines with fine, layered pigment.

From
£600
Sessions
2–3
Duration
1.5–2.5 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Beard micropigmentation works the same way as scalp density work — pigment placed between existing hairs, not over them. The result is a beard that looks deliberately shaped, whether you keep it close-cropped or longer.

We can address patchy cheeks, weak jawlines, thin sideburns and gaps where the moustache meets the beard. We can also create a soft stubble look for people who can't grow facial hair at all — worked in fine layers to look like real regrowth.

The finish is designed to blend with your natural hair colour and pattern. Nothing looks stamped on, and nothing looks like a drawn-on outline. When you shave, the base is simply closer to the skin; when you let it grow, the pigment disappears beneath the hair.

What's included

  • Facial-hair consultation
  • Colour-matching to beard hair
  • Two to three layered sessions
  • Free four-week top-up
  • Written aftercare plan
  • Shave & style as normal afterwards

Best for

  • Patchy beards on the cheeks, jaw or neck
  • Thin or uneven sideburns
  • Gaps where the moustache meets the beard
  • A soft stubble look you can't grow naturally
  • A more defined jawline without surgery or fillers
