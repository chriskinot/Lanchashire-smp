How it works

Three steps, no surprises.

From first conversation to the healed result, you always know exactly what comes next.

01

Private consultation

A relaxed 45-minute skin and brow assessment. We talk goals, tone and lifestyle, then give you an honest, fixed quote — no pressure.

02

Design & map

Your brows, lips or lash line are mapped to your facial geometry and approved by you before any pigment is applied.

03

Sessions

Two sessions, roughly four to six weeks apart, layering pigment gradually. The slower approach is why the finish looks real.

Most clients describe a light scratching sensation — closer to a fine eyebrow tweeze than a tattoo. We use a topical numbing agent before and during each session, and take breaks whenever you need them.

Brows typically last 18–24 months before a colour refresh, lips 2–3 years, and lash lines 2–3 years. A six-week top-up is included in your treatment price to perfect the healed result.

Microblading uses a fine blade to draw individual hair strokes — best for normal-to-dry skin and a very natural finish. Powder brows use a machine to layer soft shading through the whole brow — better for oily skin and a slightly more "makeup" look. Combination brows blend both.
