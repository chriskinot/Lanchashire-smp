Treatment 01

Powder Brows

Soft, shaded brow definition with a diffused, makeup-like finish — the "just filled in" look, permanently.

From
£395
Sessions
2
Duration
2.5–3 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Powder brows use a digital machine to deposit thousands of tiny pixels of pigment into the brow — building a soft, diffused wash of colour rather than individual strokes. The result is similar to a brow that's been filled in with powder or a pencil, but it doesn't wash off.

It suits almost every skin type, including oily or mature skin where microblading may not heal as crisply. The finish is more "makeup-like" than hair-stroke work, but when mapped and shaded well it reads naturally at conversational distance.

Healing takes seven to ten days, with a colour refresh at six weeks. The pigment settles to roughly 30–40% lighter than the day-of result, which is why we work a shade or two deeper in the first session.

What's included

  • Consultation & brow mapping
  • Bespoke pigment matching
  • Two sessions (initial + 6-week touch-up)
  • Aftercare balm & written plan
  • Aftercare check-in
  • Free colour refresh at 12–18 months

Best for

  • Oily or combination skin where microblading blurs
  • Mature skin with fine lines through the brow
  • Sparse or over-plucked brows
  • Anyone wanting a soft, makeup-style finish
  • Longevity — 18–24 months before a refresh

Treatment 02

Microblading

Fine hair strokes, hand-drawn to follow your natural brow pattern — ideal for sparse or over-plucked brows.

From
£395
Sessions
2
Duration
2.5–3 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Microblading is the manual counterpart to powder brows: a fine blade is used to draw individual hair strokes into the skin, following the direction and pattern of your existing brow hair. The result is a lighter, more feathered finish than a machine-shaded brow.

It works best on normal-to-dry skin where strokes heal crisply. On oily skin the strokes can blur over time, so we may recommend combination or powder brows instead — we'll always advise honestly at consultation.

The strokes are mapped to your natural growth pattern, never stamped on as a template. From conversational distance, the effect is of a fuller, tidier brow with no obvious "work" — just a shape that suits you.

What's included

  • Bespoke stroke pattern
  • Two sessions (initial + 6-week touch-up)
  • Aftercare balm & written plan
  • Aftercare check-in
  • Free colour refresh at 12–18 months

Best for

  • Normal-to-dry skin

  • Over-plucked brows
  • Anyone wanting a natural hair-stroke finish
  • A softer, more delicate look than powder

Treatment 03

Combination Brows

The best of both worlds: soft hair strokes through the front, powder shading through the tail.

From
£450
Sessions
2
Duration
3–3.5 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Combination brows combine microblading hair strokes through the bulb of the brow with machine-shaded powder through the body and tail. It's the treatment we recommend most often — it heals better than microblading alone on most skin types, and ages more gracefully.

The hair strokes give a natural front edge, so the brow doesn't look "blocky" from the side. The powder shading gives depth and definition through the arch, which is where most brows thin first. Done well, the two techniques disappear into one another.

Healing and aftercare are the same as powder brows: seven to ten days of flaking and settling, a six-week colour refresh, and a twelve to eighteen month top-up when needed.

What's included

  • Strokes + shading, blended
  • Two sessions (initial + 6-week touch-up)
  • Aftercare balm & written plan

Best for

  • Thinning arch or tail
  • Anyone wanting both natural and defined
  • Those who want maximum longevity

Treatment 04

Lip Blush

A wash of natural colour that defines the lip line, evens tone and restores lost pigment.

From
£395
Sessions
2
Duration
2.5–3 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Lip blush is a soft, diffused wash of colour through the lips — not a hard liner. It defines the lip border, evens out asymmetry, restores colour lost to ageing or cold sores, and reduces the appearance of fine lines around the mouth.

We can work very subtly (a "my lips but better" look), or build up to a stronger colour over two sessions. Cool, warm and neutral tones are available — we always mix pigment to your skin tone and undertone, never straight from a bottle.

Healing follows the same pattern as brow work: the colour darkens immediately, flakes off over seven to ten days, and settles to its true tone by week four. The six-week touch-up is when we perfect any unevenness in the healed result.

What's included

  • Consultation & lip tone analysis
  • Bespoke pigment mixing
  • Two sessions (initial + 6-week touch-up)
  • Aftercare balm & written plan
  • Cold sore protocol where needed
  • Colour refresh at 18–24 months

Best for

  • Loss of natural lip colour
  • Asymmetrical or uneven lip line
  • Pale or cool-toned lips
  • Fine lines around the mouth
  • Anyone wanting a "my lips but better" look

Treatment 05

Lash Line Enhancement

Subtle definition between the lashes that makes eyes look brighter — without looking "done".

From
£250
Sessions
2
Duration
1.5–2 hrs
Enquire about this treatment

Lash line enhancement is a soft pigment application between the lash roots, along the upper lid margin. It doesn't replace a full eyeliner flick — it deepens the base of the lashes so the eyes look brighter, wider and more awake.

It's a good option for people who wear contact lenses, have sensitive eyes or watery eyes (though we'll check suitability first), and it heals more softly than a full eyeliner tattoo. Results typically last two to three years.

We work very conservatively in the first session — the aim is a subtle, natural-looking line that doesn't dominate. If you want a bolder effect, we can build it up at the six-week touch-up.

What's included

  • Consultation & eye assessment
  • Bespoke line mapping
  • Two sessions (initial + 6-week touch-up)
  • Aftercare drops & written plan
  • Contact-lens suitability check
  • Refresh at 2–3 years

Best for

  • Sparse or pale lash lines
  • Watery or sensitive eyes
  • Contact lens wearers
  • Anyone wanting a "no-makeup" bright-eyed look
  • A subtler alternative to eyeliner tattoo
